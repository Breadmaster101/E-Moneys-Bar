Four ways to take the modern out

The same slice of the waiting room, four times. Layout, spacing and content are identical in all four — only the surface treatment changes, so what you're comparing is the direction itself.

Showing the three areas you picked: surfaces & panels, the table & felt, and atmosphere. Cards are left out since you kept those as they are.

01 Vintage screen-print poster Flat ink, off-register shadows, halftone dots. Zero blends. The loudest and most confident of the four — and the furthest from where you are now.
02 Engraved / letterpress Nothing filled, everything drawn. Shading is crosshatch, framing is double rules and corner marks. Extends the icon language you already have to the whole UI.
03 Painted saloon sign Flat paint with wear on top, gold lettering with a hard shadow, wood and brass as flat blocks. Warmest and the most obviously "a place" rather than an interface.
04 Flattened noir Today's palette and layout with every gradient collapsed, every glow removed, every soft shadow hardened. Safe — but closest to the thing you said feels too modern.
